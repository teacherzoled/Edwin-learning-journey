Learning Journey voice clips

This page asks Azure to say every phrase the apps use, one at a time, and packs the clips into one zip file. Open it in Microsoft Edge or Chrome on a computer.

  1. Paste KEY 1 and check the voice, accent and speed.
  2. Press Start and leave this tab open. Azure's free tier allows about 20 clips a minute, so the full set takes about .
  3. When it reaches the end, press Download zip and send that file to Claude.

You can pause, close the tab or lose the connection. Finished clips are kept in this browser, and Start carries on from where it stopped. Your key is not saved.

Changing the voice, accent or speed after some clips are made would mix two styles. To change, press Start over first.
