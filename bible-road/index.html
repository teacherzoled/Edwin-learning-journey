Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.Psalm 119:105 (NKJV)

Parent note: In the parable of the lost son (Luke 15), Jesus teaches that God wants to forgive both the one who ran far away and the one who thinks he is good. The heart of the story is the patient father, who keeps loving both sons and goes out to bring them home. "Good kids" need God's forgiveness too.

Parent note: Luke 1. Zechariah and Elizabeth were old and had prayed for a child. God answered and kept His promise: John was born, and he got the people ready for the coming of the Lord. Ask him: What did God promise Zechariah? Did God keep His promise?
